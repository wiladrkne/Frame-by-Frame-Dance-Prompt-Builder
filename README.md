🕺 Frame-by-Frame Dance Prompt Builder
Turn your favorite dance moves into movie magic! Whether you love Ballet or Breakdancing, this tool helps you build fun prompts for Meta AI. It’s like being a digital choreographer—just pick a move, lock in your character, and start the show!

✨ How to Play
Pick a Dancer: Choose who is on stage (like a Ballerina or a Chrome Robot).

Choose a Move: Pick a dance style, from a "Shuffle Step" to "Gravity-Defying Flips."

Add Some Magic: Pick a cool place (like a Neon Desert) and some pretty lighting.

Make it Happen: Click Generate, copy the text, and paste it into Meta AI to see your dance come to life!

🎒 Fun Features
Character DNA: Keep your dancer looking the same in every single video.

Camera Fun: Change the view from a "Bird's Eye" look to a "Close Up."

Color Palettes: Paint your scene with "Synthwave Pink" or "Bioluminescent Green."

Easy to Use: No sign-ups or internet needed once you have the file!

🚀 Get Started
Download: Save the index.html file to your computer.

Open: Just double-click it to open in your favorite web browser.

Create: Start mixing and matching to see what you can imagine!
